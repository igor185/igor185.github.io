Machine Learning Engineer with 6+ years of experience, including 2+ years as an ML Lead, specializing in **Computer Vision, MLOps and Embedded AI**. I am passionate about transforming cutting-edge AI research into real-world products that reduce costs, improve efficiency, and create new customer experiences.

Alongside my industry work, I lecture on **Generative Computer Vision** at the [Ukrainian Catholic University](https://ucu.edu.ua/en/faculty-of-applied-sciences/), speak on AI-related conferences, and mentor master's theses, helping future ML engineers grow their expertise and bring fresh ideas into the field.

## Research interests

- **Video Object Segmentation** — global motion understanding, flow-guided deformable transformers
- **Embedded AI & Edge Deployment** — model compression (distillation, pruning, quantization), real-time inference on Raspberry Pi and Hailo NPUs
- **Generative Computer Vision** — image inpainting, feature-space refinement
